Real physics

Clicks pass through

Only the charm itself takes clicks. Everything else goes straight to what's underneath, and it never takes focus from your app.

Menu bar only

Good questions.

Do the powers close my apps?

No. A double-click hides the front app, just like ⌘H. Bring it back from the Dock or with ⌘Tab. Shake effects are paint only and never touch your windows.

Why does it ask for Screen Recording?

It's optional. With it, the window that flies into the charm is a snapshot of the real one. Without it, it's a card with the app's icon and name. Turn it on from ✦ → Allow Window Snapshots…, then relaunch. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Will it slow my Mac down?

It draws in about a millisecond a frame, and while a charm hangs still or a screen effect holds, it does next to nothing.

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

DeskCharm isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open a downloaded copy macOS says it can't verify it. Click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Or install from Terminal, which skips the warning.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac on macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

How do I quit it?

✦ in the menu bar → Quit.
