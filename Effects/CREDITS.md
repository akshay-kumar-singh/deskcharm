# Effects artwork

## Bat-1.png … Bat-6.png

One wingbeat of a bat seen face on, made from a single silhouette by hinging
each wing about the edge of the body: 40° up, sweeping to 28° down and back.

- Source: [Bat shadow black.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bat_shadow_black.svg),
  as rendered to PNG by Wikimedia
- Licence: public domain

## HulkModel.glb

A rigged, animated giant — idle, a jump that ends in a two-fisted slam, punches,
a run and a death — unchanged; its own texture is replaced at load by
HulkSkin.png.

- Source: [Giant Mutant](https://opengameart.org/node/153669)
- Author: Eldritch Grim, OpenGameArt. Credit is asked for, though optional.
- Licence: [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The author
  says the animations were made with Mixamo.

## HulkSkin.png

The giant's own texture, repainted as the Hulk by mapping its light and shade
onto green skin, torn purple shorts and dark hair.

- Made from the texture in HulkModel.glb, so CC0 like it.

The Hulk himself is Marvel's design, so personal use only, like the charm art.
